The edge was real for sixteen years. It is dying.

finLM is a research stack for Indian equities: 16 years of point-in-time NSE data, a deterministic analysis layer exposed to language models, and a risk gate that refuses to place orders. The headline finding is a decay, not a return.

What has and has not been run

This is research infrastructure and a governance layer, not a live trading system. Being exact about that, because the distinction is the whole point:

  • REALBacktests over 2010-2026 NSE data, point-in-time and survivorship-verified
  • REAL13 decisions journaled with their reasoning, scored against actual NSE closes
  • BUILTBroker integration implemented and unit-tested against a mock
  • ZEROOrders ever placed. Not one.
  • NEVERBroker account authenticated. No session token has ever existed.
  • NONECapital at risk, at any point

No order was placed because the mandate file authorising one was deliberately never written, so the gate refused every time. That is the design working rather than a missing feature. Where a return figure appears, it is a paper decision scored against real closing prices: real data, real forward scoring, no money.

01Chasing loses. Fading worked, and is fading.

The scanner finds stocks that spiked on unusual volume. You can chase the move or fade it. Across 16 complete years, chasing lost in every configuration tested (-1.55% per trade net, n = 1,033). Fading produced positive benchmark-adjusted alpha in every single year.

But the effect decays monotonically, from roughly 2.3% early in the sample to 0.79% in 2025. A structural-break test put the best split before 2023, when T+1 settlement completed, an event named in the pre-registration before the data was examined. At p = 0.0375 it fails the robustness check, so it is reported as suggestive and not promoted.

The sign test is now 16 of 16 positive, p = 1/65,536: thirty-two times more "significant" than the original result, while the effect size fell about 78%. A sign test measures whether the sign is real and says nothing about magnitude. The honest headline is the decay, not the p-value.

02Most published anomalies do not show up here

Eleven documented equity anomalies, tested on NSE 2010-2024 with standard cross-sectional quintile sorts: 290,651 symbol-months, 161 usable months. In the liquid tradeable universe, essentially nothing survives. Widen the universe and two effects appear and hold.

Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.10 leaves three survivors. A bootstrap of the maximum |t| under the null (returns permuted within month, 1,000 iterations) gives observed 3.374 against a null 95th percentile of 2.828, p = 0.012. The strict test passes.

Two things need reading carefully. Skewness is statistically significant but not tradeable: its significance is significance of losing money, and flipped to the profitable direction it is worth about +0.8% a year, which is nil. And short-term reversal is exactly null at monthly horizon, which does not contradict finding 01 so much as locate it: reversal in India lives at the daily horizon.

A conditional double sort, ranking each characteristic within liquidity terciles, confirms these are not liquidity in disguise. Magnitudes shrink by about a third; significance survives.

The cost hurdle, drawn

A strategy with no edge at all, rebalancing monthly at 80% turnover, loses 6.6% a year to costs. Every finding on this page has to clear that line before it means anything.

03The gate, and the four ways it broke

An LLM handed an order tool and told to "always ask first" is not governed. The reference LLM trading server in India gates real orders behind a confirmed=true parameter that the model sets itself. That is theatre.

finLM puts authorisation in a file the human writes, which no code path in the package can create, plus a filesystem kill switch and no bypass parameter. Then it was attacked on the assumption the model is adversarial. It lost four times:

BypassHow it worked
Price spoofingA market order valued from a caller-supplied price of 1.00 cleared a 50,000 cap while executing at 500,000
Negative quantity-100 passes the quantity cap and makes value negative, clearing both caps at once
Unicode evasionA zero-width space or full-width characters slid past the symbol blocklist
NaN priceEvery comparison with NaN is false, so the value cap silently passed

All four are one bug wearing four hats: the gate trusted caller-supplied values as ground truth. That is the classic confused deputy problem, a program holding authority the caller lacks, tricked into using it because it believed the caller's description of the request.

The claim is not that the gate is unbypassable. It is that it was attacked, it broke, it was fixed, and there is a suite that keeps it shut.

04Why the numbers are trustworthy

The methodology is the actual product. Entries are at the next day's open, because end-of-day data means you cannot act on today's close, and the scanner filters the panel to prior rows structurally rather than by discipline.

Survivorship is excluded and verified rather than asserted. The universe is built from per-day exchange archives, so each row is what actually traded that day. Of 2,543 symbols across 2010-2020, 771 stop appearing before mid-2020, and the dead end at their real demise: Satyam in July 2013, Educomp in 2017, Gitanjali in July 2018, Jet Airways in September 2019, Unitech in March 2020. A symbol list backfilled from today cannot produce that pattern.

Every result is benchmark-adjusted against an equal-weight liquid-universe index, costs are modelled rather than mentally discounted, and corporate actions are back-adjusted with the full audit trail queryable. A zero-alpha strategy at 80% monthly turnover loses 6.6% a year to costs alone, and that is the bar every finding above has to clear.

Hypotheses are pre-registered

Before the data was computed, a file recorded a prediction interval and an explicit falsification region for a hypothesis about why alpha varies. The observation landed outside the interval, in the direction opposite the prediction. The hypothesis was recorded as dead. The same discipline, including a stopping rule fixed in advance, governs the multiple-testing correction and the decay analysis.

Runs are stamped

Every result-producing run writes an artifact recording the commit and whether the tree was dirty, a database fingerprint, library versions and the full config. There is no opt-out flag, and a test asserts there never will be. Documentation cites the artifact rather than retyping the number.

05What was wrong, and how it was found

The correction trail is the part worth reading. Three entries, escalating in rigour.

Moves backed by a genuine news catalyst behave differently, so take no action on those Caught after the fact. The scorer contradicted the recorded hypothesis three days later, on n = 5.
935 trades, 52.6% win rate, +1.2% alpha per trade, profit factor 1.18 Caught by audit. Would not reproduce. 18 configurations tested; the entry logic and exit defaults were byte-identical to the commit that produced it, so the run itself was never captured. A provenance layer now makes that impossible.
Market breadth explains why fade alpha varies (r = -0.64) Caught in advance. Pre-registered with its falsification region written down first, then falsified: the model predicted +2.80% with a 95% interval of [+1.57, +4.04], and the observation was +0.58%. Directionally wrong, not just outside.

Superseded figures are kept struck through with a dated correction rather than quietly replaced. Deleting them would have been easier and much worse.

06Limits worth knowing before you trust any of it

  • SHORTAbout 97% of the measured edge is short-side. An Indian retail cash account cannot hold a multi-day short, so this is a de-risking filter and not a return strategy.
  • DECAYThe edge reached 0.79% in 2025. Extrapolate the slope and it hits zero within a few years.
  • SIZENo market capitalisation data exists in the source, so there is no value-weighting and no true size factor.
  • EODEnd-of-day data only. No spreads, no order book, no execution modelling.
  • OPENFactor attribution has not been run. Until it is, momentum and 52-week-high are candidate findings and not established alpha.
